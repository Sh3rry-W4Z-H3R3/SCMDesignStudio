## Westgarth Auto Content Mapping

### 1. Header and Navigation
- SCM Studio Logo
- Navigation menu (Home, About, Services, Projects, Contact)

### 2. Hero Section
- Title: "Westgarth Auto Services 2021"
- Description: "A case study on crafting a brand identity and digital presence for a local, family-owned auto repair garage in Dunfermline."
- Call to action button: "Explore the Process"

### 3. Project Overview
- **The Client**: A local, family-owned, well-respected Garage in Dunfermline in need of a small rebrand to reflect its shift in operations and ownership.
- **The Challenge**: Create a modern refresh of the company's website to better reflect the changes taking place in the company, design new signage, and produce digital assets for future marketing campaigns.
- **The Approach**: Design signage, develop brand assets, and create a responsive website that showcases opening times and services.

### 4. Discovery & Research
- Understanding the auto repair industry and customer needs
- Research into effective signage design for auto services
- Analysis of user behavior for auto repair websites

### 5. Brand Strategy
- **Brand Positioning**: Westgarth Auto Services positioned as a modern, family-owned, trusted local garage
- **Core Values**: Reliability, professionalism, transparency, local service

### 6. Logo Development
- Concept exploration focusing on automotive themes
- Final logo design incorporating car imagery with modern typography

### 7. Brand Identity System
- **Color Palette**: Blue, red, and white color scheme (as seen in signage)
- **Typography**: Clean, professional fonts for legibility and modern appearance

### 8. Website Strategy & Planning
- **Site Objectives**: Showcase opening times prominently, provide information about services, build trust through reviews, highlight work examples
- **User Journeys**: Designed for both mobile and desktop users with responsive layout

### 9. Website Design Process
- **Wireframing & UI Design**: 2-column layout transitioning to 1-column for mobile
- **Key Design Decisions**: 
  - Prominent display of opening hours
  - Integration of review section for customer trust
  - Image slider to showcase work
  - Responsive design for all devices
  - Geolocation access through Google Maps integration

### 10. Final Sections
- Call to action: "Ready to Transform Your Ideas into Reality?"
- Contact information
- Footer with social media links
