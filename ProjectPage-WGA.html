Westgarth Auto Services - 2024

Branding | Brand Design

Digital | Web Design, Web Development

Print | Graphic Design, Signage

Context: A local, family owned, well respected Garage in Dunfermline were in need of a small rebrand to reflect its shift in operations and ownership. In addition, the company’s website was in need of a modern refresh to better reflect the changes taking place the comapany. SCM were tasked with designing new signage for the Garage as well as design and development of the new website. Addditionally, SCM produced digital assets to be used in future amrketing campaigns and as brand touchpoints.

Welcome Signage designed by SCM Studio. For use on site by vistors and customers.

Branding

Deliverables were provided in a new logo concept for the company as well as a rethink as to how the brand uses its colours and touch points to showcase their modern refresh and make their customers aware of their new operating times. SCM also designed their signage for welcoming visitors to their garage and for wayfinding.

Web Design

At SCM, we specialize in crafting modern and user-friendly websites that reflect the unique identity of our clients. Our work on the Westgarth Auto Services website exemplifies our commitment to excellence in web design and development.
