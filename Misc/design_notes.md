# SCM Studio Contact Page Design Notes

## Brand Style Elements
- Deep purple/gradient background with geometric shapes
- Modern, sleek look
- Clean white font
- Brand colors: deep purple, teal/turquoise, pink (in logo), white text
- Teal/green accent buttons

## Contact Page Requirements
- Email as primary contact method
- Phone number
- Social media links: Instagram, Pinterest, Spotify, LinkedIn
- Standard contact form
- FAQ section

## Design Inspiration
Based on research of best practices and the SCM Studio homepage, the contact page should:
- Maintain visual consistency with the homepage
- Use the same color scheme and geometric background
- Include clear, accessible contact information
- Feature a simple, intuitive contact form
- Include a well-organized FAQ section
- Be mobile-responsive

## Layout Structure
1. **Header Section**
   - Page title "Contact Us" or similar
   - Brief introduction text
   - Consistent with homepage header style

2. **Contact Information Section**
   - Email (primary) - prominently displayed
   - Phone number
   - Social media icons with links (Instagram, Pinterest, Spotify, LinkedIn)
   - Visual elements to make this section stand out

3. **Contact Form Section**
   - Clean, simple form with standard fields:
     - Name
     - Email
     - Subject/Topic
     - Message
   - Submit button in teal/green to match homepage buttons
   - Form validation for required fields

4. **FAQ Section**
   - Accordion-style questions and answers
   - 5-7 relevant FAQs for a design agency
   - Expandable/collapsible for better user experience
   - Styled to match the overall design

5. **Footer**
   - Consistent with homepage footer
   - Additional links if needed
   - Copyright information
