Frequently Asked Questions

What services does SCM Studio offer?

Our design services include website design, product packaging, branding, logo design, and full rebrands. We work with you from concept to launch, ensuring your vision becomes reality.

How does the design process work?

Our design process typically includes discovery, concept development, design creation, revisions, and final delivery. We maintain open communication throughout to ensure your vision is realized.

How long does a typical design project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A website design might take 4-6 weeks, while a complete rebrand could take 2-3 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What information do you need to start a project?

To get started, we need to understand your brand, target audience, project goals, timeline, and budget. The more information you provide, the better we can tailor our design to meet your needs.

Do you offer revisions to designs?

Yes, our standard packages include 2-3 rounds of revisions. We believe in collaboration and want to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final product.
