# FAQ Examples for SCM Studio Contact Page

Based on research of design agency FAQs, here are some relevant examples that could be included on the SCM Studio contact page:

1. **What services does SCM Studio offer?**
   Our design services include website design, product packaging, branding, logo design, and full rebrands. We work with you from concept to launch, ensuring your vision becomes reality.

2. **How does the design process work?**
   Our design process typically includes discovery, concept development, design creation, revisions, and final delivery. We maintain open communication throughout to ensure your vision is realized.

3. **How long does a typical design project take?**
   Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A website design might take 4-6 weeks, while a complete rebrand could take 2-3 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

4. **What information do you need to start a project?**
   To get started, we need to understand your brand, target audience, project goals, timeline, and budget. The more information you provide, the better we can tailor our design to meet your needs.

5. **Do you offer revisions to designs?**
   Yes, our standard packages include 2-3 rounds of revisions. We believe in collaboration and want to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final product.

6. **What are your payment terms?**
   We typically require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon project completion. For larger projects, we may establish a payment schedule based on project milestones.

7. **Can you work with clients remotely?**
   Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide and have established processes for remote collaboration, including video calls, shared design tools, and regular progress updates.

8. **Do you offer ongoing support after project completion?**
   Yes, we offer maintenance packages for websites and can provide ongoing design support for other projects. We're committed to your long-term success.
