Alastair Smith Ceramics

A case study on crafting a brand identity and digital presence for a master potter blending ancient techniques with modern engineering.

Explore the Process

Project Overview

The Client

Alastair Smith is a skilled potter establishing his online presence as a craftsperson who blends ancient ceramic techniques with his experience in modern engineering.

The Challenge

Create a brand identity and business website that authentically represents handcrafted ceramics while providing a seamless e-commerce experience.

The Approach

Develop a comprehensive brand strategy and visual identity that reflects the handcrafted nature of Alastair's work, followed by a website design that showcases his process and products.

Discovery & Research

Our journey began with understanding Alastair's craft, his audience, and the handmadeceramics market.

Market Analysis

We identified a gap in the market for authentic, handcrafted ceramics with a modern aesthetic. Our research showed that consumers increasingly value the story and process behind handmade products.

Through competitor analysis, we discovered opportunities to differentiate Alastair's brand by highlighting his unique combination of traditional techniques and engineering precision.

Visual Research

Our mood boards explored the intersection of organic ceramic forms and structured, engineered elements. We gathered inspiration from both traditional pottery techniques and contemporary ceramic design.

Key insights revealed the importance of showcasing the making process to build customer connection and trust in the craftsmanship.

Brand Strategy

Establishing the foundation for all design decisions.

Brand Positioning

We positioned Alastair Smith Ceramics as premium handcrafted pottery that balances artistic expression with functional design. The brand emphasizes the marriage of traditional craftsmanship with modern precision.

Core brand values identified: craftsmanship, authenticity, functionality, and heritage.

Logo Development

s

Concept Exploration

We began with sketches exploring different symbolic directions: pottery wheel motifs, clay forms, and firing process representations. Each concept aimed to capture the essence of Alastair's craft.

Client feedback guided us toward a direction that emphasized the pottery wheel symbolism, representing both tradition and the circular nature of creation.

Final Logo Design

The final logo incorporates a stylized pottery wheel symbol with clean, modern typography. The earthy colour palette references the natural materials used in ceramics.

We created multiple variations for different applications, ensuring the logo remains recognizable across digital and physical mediums.

Brand Identity System

Colour & Typography

The colour palette draws directly from the ceramics themselves—warm terracottas, earthy browns, and subtle beiges—creating a cohesive visual language that extends from product to digital experience.

Typography balances readability with personality—the display font features slightly irregular edges that suggest handcrafting without sacrificing legibility, while the supporting text employs a clean sans-serif.

Website Strategy & Planning

Creating a digital experience that showcases craftsmanship while enabling commerce.

Site Objectives

We identified three primary objectives for the website: showcase Alastair's craftsmanship and process, sell his ceramic products, and tell his story as an artisan.

User journeys were mapped for different visitor types, from casual browsers to serious collectors, ensuring each path led to meaningful engagement.

Website Design Process

Wireframing & UI Design

We began with wireframes that established a clear visual hierarchy and user flow. The structure evolved based on user needs, ensuring the most important content was prominently featured.

The UI design development emphasized creating digital elements that reflect ceramic textures and the organic nature of handcrafted pottery.

Key Design Decisions

The full-width hero image featuring Alastair's ceramic pieces against natural stone immediately communicates authenticity and craftsmanship.

The vertical flow guides visitors through a carefully orchestrated narrative: from brand identification, to product showcase, to personal story.

Small irregularities in button shapes and subtle texture overlays throughout the interface create a tactile quality that digital experiences often lack—a direct nod to the textural nature of ceramics.

Development & Implementation

Technical Approach

The responsive design maintains the integrity of the visual story across devices through thoughtful recomposition rather than mere rescaling.

Performance optimization techniques were implemented to ensure fast loading despite rich imagery, including lazy loading and image compression.

Photography & Content

Visual Storytelling

Product photography was directed to highlight ceramic textures and forms, using natural lighting to showcase the authentic qualities of each piece.

Behind-the-scenes documentation of Alastair's creation process provides transparency and builds connection with potential customers.
