Frequently Asked Questions

What services do SCM Studio offer?

At SCM Studio we're open to hearing about any project! Whether it's a physical product or something digital or software related. In addition, we can handle your website development, product packaging, branding, logo design, and full rebrands. With these services, we also offer marketing strategy and creative direction. We work with you from concept to launch, ensuring your vision becomes reality.

How does the design process work?

Our design process typically includes discovery, research, concept development, design creation, revisions, and final delivery. We maintain open communication throughout to ensure your vision is realized.

How long does a typical design project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A website design and development could take anywhere from 2-6 weeks, while a complete rebrand could take 2-3 months. Custom software development can usually take at least 9 months to deliver. We'll provide a detailed timeline in our project proposal after having done a disovery call and our initial consultation.

What information do you need to start a project?

To get started, we need to understand your brand, target audience, project goals, timeline, and budget. The more information you provide, the better we can tailor our design to meet your needs. We usually handle this with a discovery call. This is seperate to an inital consultation which is always free. Use the form above or click here to email us any inquiries

Do you offer revisions to designs?

Yes, our standard packages include 2 rounds of revisions. We believe in collaboration and want to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final product. More revisions are always possible however some major scope changes may be subject to charge.
