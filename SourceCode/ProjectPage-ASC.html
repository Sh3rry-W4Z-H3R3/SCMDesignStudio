Branding | Brand Design, Brand Guidelines, Logo Design

Context: A new potter setting up his online presence as a craftsperson using ancient techniques as well as modern engineering. SCM were tasked with design of both a brand identity (including logo) as well as a business website.

Logo Asset designed by SCM Studio. For use in 'Dark Mode' of website as well as any cardboard based packaging.

Branding

Alastair Smith is a Scottish Studio Potter. SCM helped to establish his online presence and brand identity so he could stand out in an increasingly competitive space. Deliverables were provided in a new logo concept for the company as well as a brand colour palette based on the usage of earthy tones in Alastair's own work. Brand Guidelines were made to assist in future promotional works such as on Social Media or Digital Placements. Inspiration for the logo was taken from Alastair's own past as refrence to tools used by his grandfather, a metalworker. As Alastair begins hsi won craft journey, it felt right to incoroprate this symbolism as it tied in perfectly with the history of clay and ceramics as a continuation of British Craft

Web Design

Crafting the website for Alastair Smith Ceramics was an exciting journey for us at SCM Studio. Our goal was to create an online space that mirrors the elegance and craftsmanship found in Alastair's ceramic work. We focused on designing a user experience that is both intuitive and visually captivating, ensuring visitors can easily navigate and appreciate the beauty of each piece. By applying UX theory and best practices, we handcoded the site to ensure seamless functionality and a polished aesthetic that complements Alastair's work. The result is a digital platform that not only showcases his artistry but also invites visitors to explore and connect with his creations in a meaningful way.
